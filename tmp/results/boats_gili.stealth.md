### Does Gili Trawangan have good nightlife?
Gili Trawangan is known for its lively nightlife. The island features numerous bars and beach clubs that host events with DJs and live music. Popular spots include Sama Sama Reggae Bar and Jiggy Bar, offering a mix of reggae, dance, and techno music. Parties often start around sunset and continue until late.
Most venues are located along the main strip, making it easy to hop between them. Happy hour deals are common, with drinks typically ranging from IDR 50,000 to 100,000 ($3 to $6.50).
For those seeking a quieter evening, there are beachside lounges offering a more relaxed atmosphere. Keep in mind that the island does not have motorized vehicles, so walking or cycling is the main mode of transportation at night.
### Why do foreigners prefer Gili Trawangan?
Gili Trawangan attracts foreigners due to its car-free environment, vibrant nightlife, and clear waters ideal for diving and snorkeling. Bicycles and horse-drawn carts (cidomo) are the primary modes of transportation, providing a unique, relaxed atmosphere.
The island is part of the Gili Islands in Indonesia, renowned for their coral reefs and diverse marine life, making it a favorite for underwater activities. Dining options range from local Indonesian dishes to international cuisine, with meals typically costing between 50,000-150,000 IDR ($3-10).
Numerous beachfront bars and restaurants offer a lively social scene. Accommodation varies from budget hostels to luxury resorts, catering to different preferences and budgets. Tap water is not safe to drink; bottled water is recommended.
The combination of natural beauty, entertainment, and a laid-back lifestyle makes Gili Trawangan a popular choice among travelers.
### Is it better to shop in Bali or Gili Trawangan?
Shopping in Bali generally offers more variety and options compared to Gili Trawangan. Bali has numerous markets, boutiques, and malls, such as Ubud Art Market and Seminyak Square, where you can find local crafts, designer items, and souvenirs. Prices in Bali can vary widely; for example, a handcrafted item might cost IDR 100,000-300,000 ($6.50-$20).
Gili Trawangan has a smaller selection, mainly focusing on beachwear, simple souvenirs, and local art. Prices here can be slightly higher due to transportation costs, with similar items costing about 10-20% more than in Bali. Bargaining is common in both locations, especially in markets.
For a comprehensive shopping experience with more choices and potentially better deals, Bali is preferable. However, if you're looking for convenience while enjoying beach life, Gili Trawangan offers adequate options.

## Which means of transport is the best for the route?
### Buses
Taking a bus from Bali to Gili Trawangan is cheaper than flying. Quite often, a long-distance bus is the cheapest transportation option. Buses normally make bathroom and/or lunch stops along the way.
**Things to remember before opting for a bus ride:**
  * If possible, make sure to choose higher-class buses for a more comfortable travel experience. Such buses often have an air conditioning system, soft reclining seats, Wi-Fi, bathrooms, etc.
  * As with plane tickets, buses are better booked in advance so that you can secure a good seat.
  * While booking a bus, keep in mind that delays are possible. Yet it still pays to get to the bus station at least 15 minutes prior to the scheduled departure.


### Taxi
Catching a cab is the easiest way to get from Bali to Gili Trawangan. It allows you to tailor-make your trip, plan all the detours and see all the places you want. You can choose the type and the size of your car as well to be as comfortable as you'd like to.
Depending on the route, it might cost as much as £1,042.
**Things to remember:** It's a good idea to follow the route on Google Maps or any other GPS navigator.
### Ferries
Travelling by ferry is one of the most scenic ways to get from Bali to Gili Trawangan as it combines transportation and sightseeing to some extent.
However, with these upsides in mind, you should still understand ferry travel is not for everyone. If you’re prone to seasickness it may be an unpleasant experience. As a rule, a ferry journey isn’t the cheapest way to travel During the high season ferry tickets might be hard to get, so you should always book in advance when possible.
**Things to remember:** ferry rides are dependent on weather conditions.
### Vans
A shared van is another means of transport for traveling from Bali to Gili Trawangan. As far as travel time is concerned, it’s basically equal to a taxi ride, while price-wise it’s way more affordable. The majority of tourists book a van online in advance.
Sometimes, when trains or buses aren’t available, a van is your only way to get from Bali to Gili Trawangan.
Things to remember: while traveling by van, you might not have as much space for luggage as you would on a train or a plane. It’s a good idea to pack only the most useful things for a van trip and put them in a backpack that will be easy to carry around..
